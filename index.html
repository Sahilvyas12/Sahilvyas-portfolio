About

A little about me

I work in operations. The part I enjoy most is the stuff that doesn't have a playbook yet - where the problem is fuzzy, nobody owns it fully, and someone has to figure out what "done" even looks like.

That usually means sitting between teams, finding what's actually slowing things down, and turning a rough idea into a process people can run.

I'm most interested in business operations, process improvement, strategy, research, cross-functional execution, and building things from zero.

My operating principles

01

Find the bottleneck.

Understand what is actually slowing the system down before trying to fix it.

02

Start with the smallest useful version.

Don't overbuild before proving that something works.

03

Measure what matters.

Focus on outcomes and operational metrics, not activity.

04

Own the problem.

If something is broken, understand it end-to-end instead of waiting for someone else to fix it.
